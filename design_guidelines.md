# Luxarna Hotel & Spa - Design Guidelines

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Luxarna Hotel & Spa
- **Tagline**: Your Peaceful Escape Awaits
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, modern, calm, elegant with dynamic motion
- **Color Palette**: Rich gold accents, elegant deep blues, champagne tones, warm highlights, subtle gradients
- **Overall Vibe**: Lively luxury - sophisticated yet inviting, peaceful yet dynamic

## Design System Approach
Reference-based approach drawing from luxury hospitality leaders (Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton web presence) with emphasis on:
- Spacious layouts with breathing room
- Elegant typography hierarchy
- Smooth, purposeful animations
- Premium visual treatment

## Typography & Spacing
- **Spacing Units**: Use Tailwind units of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24 for consistent rhythm
- **Typography Hierarchy**: 
  - Hero headlines: Large, elegant serif or sophisticated sans-serif
  - Section headers: Clear hierarchy with generous spacing
  - Body text: Highly readable with comfortable line-height
  - Pricing: Bold, prominent display

## Layout Structure

### Homepage
- **Hero Section**: Full-width luxury hero with hotel exterior image as background, overlay gradient for text legibility
- **Hero Content**: Centered tagline with fade-in animation, prominent CTAs with blur backgrounds
- **Section Previews**: Grid layout showcasing Rooms, Spa, Restaurant & Karaoke, About with smooth hover effects
- **CTAs**: "Book Your Stay" and "Explore Rooms" buttons with dynamic hover animations
- **Footer**: Contact information and location details

### Rooms Page
- **Layout**: Modern grid displaying 4 room types:
  1. Deluxe King Suite - ₦45,000
  2. King Suite - ₦35,000
  3. Deluxe Room - ₦25,000
  4. Standard Room - ₦20,000
- **Card Structure**: Room image (use provided interior photos), title, price, description, "Check Availability" button
- **Interaction**: Smooth hover animations on cards and buttons

### Spa Page
- **Services Display**: Clean, calm layout with:
  - Full Body Massage - ₦45,000
  - Pedicure - ₦7,500
  - Manicure - ₦7,500
  - Facials - ₦18,000
- **Visual Treatment**: Soft gradients, balanced spacing, serene atmosphere

### Restaurant & Karaoke Bar Page
- **Meal Times Display**:
  - Breakfast: 7:00 AM – 10:30 AM
  - Lunch: 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM
  - Dinner: 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM
- **Layout**: Elegant presentation with menu placeholder and photo placeholders

### About Page
- **Content**: Luxarna story and core values (comfort, privacy, luxury)
- **Design**: Elegant typography with subtle animations, generous whitespace

### Contact Page
- **Elements**: Contact form, WhatsApp icon integration, phone/email display, address (Plot 13, Trunk C, Mandela Estate), map placeholder
- **Layout**: Clean, accessible form design

## Navigation
- **Style**: Fully responsive with animated buttons
- **Menu Items**: Menu, Book Now, Contact, Rooms, Spa
- **Interaction**: Smooth hover effects, lively and responsive feel

## Animations & Interactions
- **Hero**: Smooth fade-in effects on load
- **Buttons**: Dynamic hover animations with subtle transformations
- **Cards**: Smooth hover effects with elevation changes
- **Transitions**: Soft, elegant transitions throughout (0.3s-0.5s duration)
- **Scroll**: Subtle scroll-triggered animations for section reveals

## Component Library
- **Buttons**: Luxury-styled with blur backgrounds when over images, gold/champagne accent colors, smooth hover states
- **Cards**: Clean borders or subtle shadows, hover lift effects
- **Forms**: Elegant inputs with focus states, accessible labels
- **Icons**: Professional icon set for amenities, services, contact methods

## Images
- **Homepage Hero**: Hotel exterior image (luxarna-hotel-and-s-ng-port-harcourt) as full-width background with overlay
- **Rooms Section**: Two interior room photos (room pics 2 and Room pics) showcasing luxury accommodations
- **Other Sections**: Placeholders for restaurant, spa, amenities

## Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop**: Full layout with multi-column grids where appropriate
- **Tablet**: Adjusted grid layouts (2-column for rooms)
- **Mobile**: Single-column stacking, touch-friendly buttons, maintained luxury feel

## Key Principles
- **Premium Feel**: Every element should exude quality and attention to detail
- **Dynamic Motion**: Lively animations that enhance, not distract
- **Clear Hierarchy**: Users should immediately understand page structure and CTAs
- **Accessibility**: SEO-friendly headings, readable contrast, clear navigation
- **Performance**: Fast loading with optimized images and smooth animations
